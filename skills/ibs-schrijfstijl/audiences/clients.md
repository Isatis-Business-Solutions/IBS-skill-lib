# Clients and prospects

Propositions, business cases, offers (offertes), client e-mails, project updates.

**Reader:** decision makers and owners at clients and prospects (finance, data, IT, management). They want
to be sure we understand their problem, will deliver, and are honest about cost and risk.

**Default mix**

| Leading | Present | Undertone |
|---|---|---|
| ownership, openness | inventiveness, curiosity | lightness |

**Adjust to the goal**
- First contact or introduction: curiosity → leading, ownership → present.
- Delay, problem or escalation: openness and ownership stay leading; inventiveness → undertone.
- Workshop invite or new idea: inventiveness → leading, ownership → present.

**Shape:** their problem in their words → what we do → what it delivers → what it costs and requires → next
step with a date.

**Address:** u or je, following the existing relationship; if unknown, ask.
