# Candidates

Vacancies, recruitment posts, employer branding, messages to potential colleagues.

**Reader:** data, finance and IT professionals deciding whether IBS is a place for them. They want to feel
the people and the culture, and to know what the work really is.

**Default mix**

| Leading | Present | Undertone |
|---|---|---|
| lightness, curiosity, inventiveness | openness | ownership |

**Adjust to the goal**
- Vacancy text: openness → leading for the hard facts (assignments, hours, location, terms), lightness stays
  leading in the framing.
- Story about a colleague: curiosity leading; let the colleague's own words carry it.

**Consistency:** recruitment promises what colleagues already experience. Write nothing a current colleague
would not recognise.

**Shape:** who you would work with and on what → what it is like → the facts → how to get in touch (low
threshold).

**Address:** je/jij/jullie.
