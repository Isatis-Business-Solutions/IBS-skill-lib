# Inventiveness — leading

**Feel: surprising, sharp, original.**
- *Surprising*: the reader meets an angle they did not expect, and it makes them read on.
- *Sharp*: the angle reveals the point faster or deeper than a plain statement would.
- *Original*: it could only have been written for this subject; it is not borrowed or stock.

**Rules**
- The text has one central idea or image, chosen for this subject, and carries it through.
- The opening is the most inventive part.
- Form may bend when it serves the point.
- Each fresh element must make the message clearer, never only decorate it.

**Boundary:** surprising, never forced. One idea, not a pile of them; the core message stays clear in the
first lines.
