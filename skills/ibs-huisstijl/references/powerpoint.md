# PowerPoint decks (.pptx)

Use a pptx skill or library for the mechanics; this file gives the look. **Slides are dark** (16:9).

- **Theme colours:**
  - dark 1 `#171717`, light 1 `#FEF9ED`;
  - dark 2 `#232323`, light 2 `#9A958C`;
  - accent 1 `#F9C852`, accent 2 `#C9A24A`, accent 3 `#7FB77E`, accent 4 `#8FB3D9`, accent 5 `#D9725B`,
    accent 6 `#3A3A3A`.
- **Theme fonts:** Poppins Light (headings) / Poppins (body). Fallback Arial. Embed fonts if possible.
- **Masters / layouts:**
  - **Title:** dark background with a soft amber glow from the top-right (radial gradient `#613F0F` →
    transparent), `ibs-logo-on-dark.png` top-left, big Light title, eyebrow with date or occasion.
  - **Section:** big heading, small yellow square + thin double line as divider.
  - **Content:**
    - title 32–40 pt Light, top-left;
    - body 18–24 pt, at most ~5 bullets;
    - `ibs-mark-256.png` small bottom-right;
    - slide number bottom-left in muted.
  - **Big number:** one figure 96–140 pt SemiBold in yellow, label below in muted.
  - **Closing:** short message, contact, full logo.
- **Shapes:** rounded rectangles (generous radius) in `#232323` as cards; no outlines, no shadows, no 3D,
  no clip-art.
- **Charts:** theme accent order; first series yellow; subtle gridlines; labels in muted.
- **Images:** full-bleed or in rounded cards, darkened if text goes on top.
