# The skeleton

The skeleton is the order of **beats** everything else moves around. Each beat has one job: what the viewer
thinks at that moment (`thinks`), and a **phase**: tension (a question, a problem, something that pulls) or
release (the answer, the point, calm).

## From goal to skeleton

| Goal | Skeleton | Beats |
|---|---|---|
| Inform: the viewer must understand | Inverted pyramid | Core → Why it matters → Details → What now |
| Persuade: the viewer must want or do | Problem, Agitate, Solve + CTA | Problem → Agitate → Solution → Success → CTA |
| Bind to the brand: watch and come back | Cold open | Cold open → Brand → Beginning → But → Payoff |

- **And, But, Therefore** is the smallest skeleton and the test for every level: a clip, a beat, a caption.
  "And then, and then" means a *but* is missing.
- **Longer clips or several points:** keep the main skeleton and make every point its own And, But,
  Therefore (the **ABT chain**). Each *but* wins the attention back.
- **Two goals** (for example persuade, then inform): link the two parts with a *but*: the first release is
  followed by a new tension, whose release is the second part.
- **The customer is the hero, IBS the guide.** The story is about the viewer's problem; IBS appears as the
  one who helps.

## Order

- The CTA comes last. Release never before its tension. A payoff moved to the front is a cold open, and must
  return at the end with its context.
- Short clips (≤ 30 s) open with a question, a problem or the strongest image, not with context.

## Tension and release must feel different

| | Tension | Release |
|---|---|---|
| Shot length | 1.5–3 s | 3–6 s (graphics up to ~9 s) |
| Camera | handheld, push-in | locked-off, slow push-in |
| Montage | FX flash on cuts, riser | double wipe in, no flash, no riser |
| Text | the question | the answer, with time to read |

If every beat runs at the same pace, everything reads as tension.

## Reading time

A caption stays at least 1 s + 0.3 s per word (max ~6 words). A graphic that explains holds its end state
at least 1.5 s after the last element lands. Sum the beats: when the clip runs long, cut a beat, not the
reading time.
