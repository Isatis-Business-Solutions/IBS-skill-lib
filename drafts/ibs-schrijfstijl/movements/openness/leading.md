# Openness — leading

The reader knows exactly where they stand. Nothing important is hidden in a subclause or an appendix.

- Put the essential facts first: what happens, when, what it costs, what changes for the reader.
- Name assumptions and uncertainty, with what we do about them.
- Explain the why behind a decision in one sentence.
- Bad news early and plainly, followed directly by what happens next.
- Numbers over adjectives: "binnen 4 weken" beats "snel", "€ 12.000" beats "scherp geprijsd".
- Plain words: no jargon that hides, no euphemisms ("uitdaging" for a problem, "aanpassing" for a cut).

**Open, never overwhelming.** Share what helps the reader decide or act. Detail goes to an appendix, a
table or a link.

> Niet: "Er zijn enkele uitdagingen rondom de planning."
> Wel: "We lopen twee weken uit, omdat de brondata later binnenkwam dan gepland. Nieuwe opleverdatum:
> 14 maart. De kosten blijven gelijk."
