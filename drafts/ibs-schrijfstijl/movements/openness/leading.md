# Openness — leading

**Feel: clear, honest, unguarded.**
- *Clear*: the reader understands their position on first read.
- *Honest*: nothing is polished beyond what is true; bad news reads as bad news.
- *Unguarded*: the text does not protect IBS at the reader's expense; assumptions and doubts are shown.

**Rules**
- The essential facts come first: what, when, cost, what changes for the reader.
- Assumptions and uncertainties are named, with what we do about them.
- The why behind a decision gets one sentence.
- Bad news comes early, directly followed by what happens next.
- Numbers and specifics replace adjectives wherever they exist.
- Plain words; no euphemism, no jargon used to hide.

**Boundary:** open, never overwhelming. Share what helps the reader decide or act; detail goes to an
appendix or a link.
