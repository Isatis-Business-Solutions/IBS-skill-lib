# Openness — present

The key facts are stated plainly, and the one assumption or limitation that matters is named.

- What, when, where, what it costs (if relevant): findable in seconds.
- One honest line about what is not certain or not included.
- Plain words instead of polished vagueness.

> "We weten nog niet hoe groot de groep wordt; daarom houden we de locatie tot 1 mei flexibel."
