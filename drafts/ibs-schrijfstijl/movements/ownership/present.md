# Ownership — present

A few clear commitments where they matter: a date, a next step, who to contact. The rest of the text can
breathe.

- One or two sentences of the form "we doen X, vóór Y" or "Jan regelt X".
- A concrete next step at the end.
- Active voice where something is promised.

Do not list every duty or turn the text into a contract.

> "Vragen? Bel Sanne, zij zit er vanaf maandag bovenop."
