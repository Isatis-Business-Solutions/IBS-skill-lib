# Inventiveness — leading

The idea is the hook. The text earns attention with an angle the reader did not expect, and that angle
carries the message.

- Open with an unexpected entry: a comparison from outside data and IT, a reversal, a small story, a
  striking number, a question nobody asks.
- Build around **one** image and carry it through; do not stack metaphors.
- Offer a solution or view the reader had not considered, and show briefly why it works.
- Play with form if it serves the point: a list of questions, a before/after, a mini dialogue.

Avoid wordplay that needs explaining and creativity that delays the point.

**Surprising, never forced.** The core message is still clear in the first lines.

> Niet: "Data is van groot belang voor uw organisatie."
> Wel: "Jullie data is een kelder vol wijn zonder etiketten: waardevol, maar niemand durft een fles open
> te trekken."
