# Curiosity — leading

The text is interested before it is interesting. It starts from the reader's world, not from IBS.

- Open with the reader's situation, in their words: their question, their Monday morning, their numbers.
- Ask real questions, ones we actually want answered. Not rhetorical filler.
- Share what we are learning or wondering, including what we do not know yet.
- Invite a reply or a conversation, and make it easy.
- Show interest in the why behind the numbers, not only the numbers.

Avoid interrogation (a few questions at most), fake questions ("Wil jij ook groeien?") and texts where every
sentence starts with "wij".

**Interested, never prying or naive.** We ask because we know enough to ask well.

> Niet: "Wij zijn specialist in business intelligence."
> Wel: "Welk rapport opent jullie CFO als eerste op maandagochtend? Daar beginnen we meestal."
