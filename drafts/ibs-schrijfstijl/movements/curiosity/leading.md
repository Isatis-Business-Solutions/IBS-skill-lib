# Curiosity — leading

**Feel: interested, attentive, open-minded.**
- *Interested*: the text cares about the reader's world before talking about IBS.
- *Attentive*: it shows it has listened; it uses the reader's situation and language.
- *Open-minded*: it is willing to wonder, to not know yet, to learn.

**Rules**
- The text starts from the reader's situation, not from IBS.
- Questions are real: we want the answer. A few at most.
- What we are learning or wondering may be shared, including what we do not know yet.
- It invites a reaction and makes responding easy.
- The reader is addressed more than IBS talks about itself.

**Boundary:** interested, never prying or naive. We ask because we know enough to ask well.
