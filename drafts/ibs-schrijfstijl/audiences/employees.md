# IBS colleagues

Internal updates, events, changes, policies, practical information.

**Reader:** IBS colleagues, often working at client sites and busy. They want to know where they stand and
what they need to do.

**Default mix**

| Leading | Present | Undertone |
|---|---|---|
| openness, ownership | lightness | curiosity, inventiveness |

**Adjust to the goal**
- Event or outing: lightness → leading, next to openness (what, when, where, what to bring, by when to sign
  up). Fun, but the practical facts are complete.
- Change, policy or difficult news: openness and ownership leading; lightness only as warmth (undertone).
- Asking for input or ideas: curiosity → leading.

**Shape:** what is happening → what it means for you → what you need to do, by when → who to ask. The first
two lines answer the first three.

**Address:** je/jij/jullie.
