# Market, peers and partners

Thought pieces, LinkedIn posts for the market, client cases, newsletters, event invitations for clients and
partners.

**Reader:** professionals in finance, data and IT who may one day work with us or recommend us. They want an
idea worth their time.

**Default mix**

| Leading | Present | Undertone |
|---|---|---|
| curiosity, inventiveness | openness, lightness | ownership |

**Adjust to the goal**
- Client case: ownership → present (what we did, the result in numbers), openness leading (what was hard).
- Event invitation: lightness → leading, openness present for the facts.

**Shape:** an observation or question → the idea → what it means in practice → an invitation to react.
