# Channel: LinkedIn

- The first two lines decide whether anyone clicks "meer". Put the hook and the point there.
- Short paragraphs of 1–3 sentences; white space between them.
- 80–200 words is usually enough. One idea per post.
- Write as a person (the poster), not as a brochure. "Ik" is fine on a personal account.
- End with a question or an easy next step. At most 3 hashtags, at the end. Emoji: 0–2.
- Tag people only with their consent and when they contributed.
