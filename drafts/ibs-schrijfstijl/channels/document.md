# Channel: document (proposition, business case, offer, report)

- Start with a summary of at most half a page: the problem, the proposal, what it delivers, what it costs,
  the decision needed.
- Headings that say something ("Wat het oplevert: 6 uur per week minder handwerk"), not labels
  ("Resultaten").
- Assumptions and risks in their own short section, never hidden.
- Numbers in tables; the reasoning in text.
- End with the next step, the owner and the date.
