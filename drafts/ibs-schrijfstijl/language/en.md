# Language: English

- Clear international English: short sentences, common words; readers are often non-native.
- "We" for IBS; "you" for the reader. Contractions are fine except in formal documents.
- Plain over corporate: "use" not "leverage", "help" not "facilitate", "start" not "commence".
- Avoid clichés: "in today's fast-paced world", "take it to the next level", "unlock value", "synergy",
  "data is the new oil", "best-in-class".
- Keep the IBS names as they are (Isatis Business Solutions, IBS); do not translate Dutch proper names.
