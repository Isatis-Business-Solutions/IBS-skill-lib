# Language: Dutch

- **Je/jij/jullie** for colleagues, candidates and the network. For clients: follow the user or the existing
  relationship; when unsure in a first formal contact, use **u**, and ask the user if it matters.
- Plain Dutch over officialese: "via" not "middels", "over" not "inzake", "om" not "teneinde", "graag" not
  "gelieve".
- **Professional jargon stays English, always.** Data, AI, IT and business terms are written the way
  professionals say them: data management, data governance, data quality, data engineer, data pipeline,
  master data, deduplication, machine learning, AI model, dashboard, business case, stakeholder. Never
  translate or half-translate them ("datapijplijn", "gegevensbeheer", "ontdubbelen", "datamanager"):
  that makes IBS sound like it does not know the field. Write them as in English (data management, not
  datamanagement); join them to a Dutch word with a hyphen (AI-toepassing, data management-team). Job
  titles in English too (Data Engineer, Data Manager).
- Everyday words around the jargon stay Dutch: "We bouwen de data pipeline", not "We build de pipeline".
- Prefer verbs over nouns: "we analyseren" not "we voeren een analyse uit".
- Avoid clichés: "ontzorgen", "in de huidige snel veranderende wereld", "naar een hoger niveau tillen",
  "toegevoegde waarde", "synergie", "data is het nieuwe goud", "proactief meedenken".
- "We" for IBS as a team; "IBS" when the company is the subject for an outsider.
