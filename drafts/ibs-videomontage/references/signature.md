# The IBS video signature

The signature has three layers. Keep them apart: what is fixed never changes, the signature moves come back
in every clip, and the rest may vary.

## Fixed
- Colours, font and logo from `ibs-huisstijl`: canvas `#171717`, accent IBS-yellow `#F9C852` (one accent),
  text cream `#FEF9ED`, muted `#9A958C`, Poppins (headings Light, numbers SemiBold, labels in tracked capitals).
- Base motion in the release is **productive**: short and quiet, ease-out, no bounce.
- 24 fps. Timing is briefed in frames.

## Signature moves
1. **Image processing as a layer, in code:** edge detection, halftone, dithering and the other looks. They
   alternate through a clip; no look repeats on two shots in a row.
2. **FX flash** on tension cuts: a few frames of a look instead of a white flash, with a hit.
3. **Double wipe** as the release transition per new topic.
4. **The IBS divider** (double line + IBS mark) as every line wipe, and the logo spin that turns into it.
5. **Kinetic captions** word by word, 3-frame stagger with overshoot, one key word in yellow.
6. **Object-detection callouts** with playful labels about the story.

## Free per clip
Subject, footage, speaker, the order of looks, the labels and the words.

## Principles
- **Tension and release.** Tension is the teaser: attention, short shots, effects. Release is the message:
  elegance. The release feels calm *because* the tension was loud; distribute effects over time instead of
  leaving them out.
- **Expressive motion belongs to important moments.** Overshoot is allowed in text and in the rotation of the
  logo spin; the logo assemble uses ease-out.
- **Synchresis:** every loud visual event that lands has a sound on the same frame (the renderer adds hits).
- **Reproducible:** use the assets as they are; change their parameters, not their design.

## Never
- A plain yellow square as the centre of a divider or wipe line.
- Hand-drawn arrows, underlines or boxes (they are for other brands).
- A white flash on a cut.
- A second accent colour, drop shadows, or the logo stretched or recoloured. The logo may rotate in motion,
  but it always lands upright.
